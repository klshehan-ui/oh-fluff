Oh, Fluff! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Oh, Fluff! is a puzzle game. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

What the game saves

Your level progress, sound setting and play statistics are saved only on your own device, using your browser's storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases them.

Feedback

The in-game "Send feedback" screen only creates text that you can choose to copy and send yourself. The game does not send it anywhere.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your device's IP address as part of a normal web request. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads and accounts

There are no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no tracking.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the Google Play store listing.
